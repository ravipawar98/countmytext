FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a character counter?

A character counter tells you how many characters are in a piece of text. It is useful for social posts, titles, applications and forms with character limits.

Does the counter include spaces?

Yes. The main character count includes spaces, while a separate statistic excludes whitespace.

Is my text uploaded or stored?

The current tool processes your text in the browser and does not require an account.
